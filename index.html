A Taste of the Ocean.

Carefully crafted seafood made with fresh ingredients and bold flavors.

Top Dishes

Pan-Seared Cedar-Plank Salmon

A thick, flaky Atlantic salmon fillet seared to a crisp golden crust, then finished on a smoking cedar plank. Served over a bed of wild rice pilaf and drizzled with a sweet bourbon-maple glaze.

Garlic Butter Butterfish

A delicate, melt-in-your-mouth white fish fillet pan-roasted in a rich, bubbling garlic and lemon-herb butter sauce. Accompanied by charred asparagus spears and creamy parmesan risotto.

Cast-Iron Seared Scallops

Jumbo wild-caught sea scallops seared in a hot cast-iron skillet to achieve a perfect, caramelized crust. Served over a vibrant sweet pea purée and finished with crispy prosciutto crumbles.
